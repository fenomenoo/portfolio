SECTION:: 01 / THE CUT LIST

Most tools answer who exists. Mine answers who changed.

npi-triggers pulls the federal US provider registry and finds healthcare organisations that were just created, or whose record just moved. Free, public, standard library only. Run it yourself.

SourceCMS NPPES, the federal public record of every US provider organisation
SignalTwo dates on every record: when it was created, and when it last changed
The catchThe API returns organisations that aren't in the state you asked for. They get discarded, not counted
BuiltUnasked, against a problem two companies described in their own job listings
